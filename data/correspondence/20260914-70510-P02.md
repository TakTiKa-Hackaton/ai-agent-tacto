# Price adjustment from 01 January 2027

letter - 2026-09-14 - Antriebstechnik Nordmark AG

Dear Sir or Madam,

we have supplied Hollerau Pumpenwerke reliably for many years. Owing to the strong rise in copper and electronic component prices, we are forced to adjust our prices for Motor IE3 7.5 kW, 4-pole (part:350011) by **6.5 %** with effect from **01 January 2027**: from EUR 348.32 to EUR 370.96 per  pc.

We regret this step and ask for your understanding.

Yours faithfully,
Antriebstechnik Nordmark AG
