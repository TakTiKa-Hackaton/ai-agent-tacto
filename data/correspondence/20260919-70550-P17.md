# Price adjustment from 01 January 2027

letter - 2026-09-19 - Platinenwerk Lausitz GmbH

Dear Sir or Madam,

we have supplied Hollerau Pumpenwerke reliably for many years. Owing to the strong rise in copper and electronic component prices, we are forced to adjust our prices for Control board HB smart pump (PCBA) (part:350030) by **8.0 %** with effect from **01 January 2027**: from EUR 34.35 to EUR 37.10 per  pc.

We regret this step and ask for your understanding.
