# Price adjustment from 01 November 2026

letter - 2026-09-11 - Gießerei Brandlhuber GmbH & Co. KG

Dear Sir or Madam,

we have supplied Hollerau Pumpenwerke reliably for many years. Owing to sustained increases in energy, raw material and wage costs, we are forced to adjust our prices for Volute casing HN 50-200 (part:310010) by **9.5 %** with effect from **01 November 2026**: from EUR 126.53 to EUR 138.55 per  pc.

We regret this step and ask for your understanding.

Yours faithfully,
Gießerei Brandlhuber GmbH & Co. KG
