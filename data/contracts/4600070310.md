# Framework agreement 4600070310

Between Hollerau Pumpenwerke GmbH and Blechtechnik Aichbauer GmbH.

**§ 2.1** Subject: supply of Base frame HN 50/65, welded, painted.

**§ 3.1** Prices as per the price annex, fixed for each calendar year. Changes require written notice of at least 90 days before the end of the calendar year.

**§ 4.1** Payment: 45 days net.

**§ 5.1** Delivery DAP; standard lead time 21 days from order.

**§ 6.1** The quality agreement applies: on-time delivery target 95 %, flat fee of EUR 150 per supplier-caused complaint.

**§ 8.1** Valid from 2025-07-01 to 2027-06-30; extends by 12 months unless terminated with 90 days' notice.
