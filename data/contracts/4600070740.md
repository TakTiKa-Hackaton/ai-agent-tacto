# Framework agreement 4600070740

Between Hollerau Pumpenwerke GmbH and Blankstahl Werdenfels GmbH.

**§ 2.1** Subject: supply of Round bar 1.4404 dia 30 mm, peeled, Round bar 1.4021 dia 40 mm, peeled, Round bar C45 dia 50 mm, cold drawn.

**§ 3.1** Prices as per the price annex, fixed for each calendar year. Changes require written notice of at least 60 days before the end of the calendar year.

**§ 3.2** The price follows index PPI-C2431: P = P0 x (1 - 0.8 + 0.8 x I(m-2) / I(base)), base = the index two months before the price year starts, adjusted quarterly on the 1st of January, April, July and October if the change exceeds 3 %.

**§ 4.1** Payment: 30 days net.

**§ 5.1** Delivery DAP; standard lead time 14 days from order.

**§ 6.1** The quality agreement applies: on-time delivery target 95 %, flat fee of EUR 150 per supplier-caused complaint.

**§ 8.1** Valid from 2024-01-01 to 2026-12-31; extends by 12 months unless terminated with 60 days' notice.

Parts: part:370010, part:370011, part:370012
