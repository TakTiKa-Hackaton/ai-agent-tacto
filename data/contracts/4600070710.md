# Framework agreement 4600070710

Between Hollerau Pumpenwerke GmbH and Edelstahl-Service Donautal GmbH.

**§ 2.1** Subject: supply of Round bar 1.4404 dia 30 mm, peeled.

**§ 3.1** Prices as per the price annex, fixed for each calendar year. Changes require written notice of at least 60 days before the end of the calendar year.

**§ 3.2** The price follows index AS-14404: P = P0 x (1 - 0.45 + 0.45 x I(m-2) / I(base)), base = the index two months before the price year starts, adjusted quarterly on the 1st of January, April, July and October if the change exceeds 0 %.

**§ 4.1** Payment: 30 days net.

**§ 5.1** Delivery DAP; standard lead time 10 days from order.

**§ 6.1** The quality agreement applies: on-time delivery target 95 %, flat fee of EUR 150 per supplier-caused complaint.

**§ 8.1** Valid from 2024-01-01 to 2026-12-31; extends by 12 months unless terminated with 60 days' notice.
